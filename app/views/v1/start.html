Commercial Value Dashboard

Track and compare the value of your procurement activity.

What you'll get

  • Value comparison and analysis – view your contract values and savings performance
  • Benchmarking insights – Compare your procurement activity with similar procurements and peer organisations
  • Detailed reporting – Graphical data visualisations and exportable summaries of your commercial value results

How it works

  • Access the service through Welcome to GOV.UK One Login – more information about GOV.UK One Login
  • Once you register, any contract award notice data that is known about your organisation from the central digital platform will be pre-populated – more information about the central digital platform (CDP)
  • Input your savings data – provide some details about your awarded contracts using our guided form or template file upload
  • View your results and get instant data insights
  • Download your report – save and share your findings

What you'll need

You'll need to access this service using your GOV.UK One Login , and connect this service to your organisation's CDP notice data using your PPON buyer ID, or similar.

Have your recently awarded contract details and associated savings methodologies to hand. Your savings information needs to include your baseline approach and baseline value.

Time to complete

First time sign up: unknown

Report your savings data: 5-10 minutes, depending on the amount of data you wish to report

You can view and sort your data insights instantly, and return to view them at any time.
