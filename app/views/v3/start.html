Commercial Value Tool

You can use this service to:

  • track, compare and analyze the value of your contract values and savings performance
  • benchmark insights that compares your procurement activity with similar procurements and peer organisations
  • view and share detailed reporting and graphical data visualisations and exportable summaries of your commercial value results

Before you start

To use this service, you will need:

  • a GOV.UK One Login
  • contract award notice data from the Central Digital Platform (CDP)
  • details of any direct cash savings made against your contract budget
  • details of any price protections, budget freezes, or performance benefits achieved
