Commercial Value Tool

Use this service to record and compare the value created through your contracts.

This service is for commercial and procurement professionals at contracting authorities.

What you can do

  • record savings and benefits from awarded contracts
  • compare your procurement activity with similar organisations
  • view, share and export reports about your commercial value

Before you start

You will need:

  • a GOV.UK One Login
  • contract award notice data associated with theCentral Digital Platform (CDP)
  • details of any cash savings made against your budget
  • details of any price protections, fixed prices or performance benefits you achieved
