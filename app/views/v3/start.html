Commercial Value Tool

This service is for commercial and procurement professionals at contracting authorities who need to record and understand the value created through their contracts.

What you can do

  • record savings and other benefits from awarded contracts
  • compare your organisation's procurement activity with similar procurements and peer organisations
  • view, share and export reports about your organisation's commercial value

Before you start

To use this service, you will need:

  • a GOV.UK One Login
  • contract award notice data from the Central Digital Platform (CDP)
  • details of any direct cash savings made against your contract budget
  • details of any price protections, budget freezes, or performance benefits achieved
